Working late, or just want more on the screen? Here you choose light or dark, and how compact HappyVibe is, from roomy to ultra-compact. In the app's own words: "How HappyVibe looks. Changes apply at once, in every window."

## Where to find it

**App features** → **Appearance** in the sidebar.

## What's on the screen

### Theme

"Light or dark. The terminal keeps its own colours, under Terminal." Three choices, each with a small sketch:

- **Light**, the default: "Cream paper and warm ink."
- **Dark**: "The same warm colours on a dark background, easier on the eyes at night."
- **Match system**: "Light or dark like the rest of your computer, and switches when it does."

### Density

"How much room the app gives everything. Only Ultra-compact makes text smaller." Three choices, each with a small sketch of the difference:

- **Comfortable**, the default: "Room around everything, and the conversation in a reading-width column."
- **Compact**: "Tighter spacing everywhere, and the conversation uses more of the window. Text keeps its size."
- **Ultra-compact**: "Compact, with text a size smaller too, to fit more on the screen."

## Switch to dark

1. Open **Appearance**.
2. Under **Theme**, click **Dark**.

The whole app turns dark at once: the sidebar, the conversation, the settings pages, the dialogs and the file editor. Every open window follows, and HappyVibe remembers the choice the next time it starts. Click **Light** to go back.

Code blocks stay dark, a shade deeper than the page, so they still stand out. The terminal keeps the style you picked under **Terminal**. An HTML file's preview stays white, the way a browser shows it. The **User guide** stays light too: it shows the guide's website, which has one look.

Pick **Match system** to let your computer decide. If it switches to dark in the evening, HappyVibe switches with it, without a restart.

## Make HappyVibe more compact

1. Open **Appearance**.
2. Under **Density**, click **Compact**, or **Ultra-compact** for even more on the screen.

The whole app tightens at once: the sidebar, the tab strip, the settings pages and the conversation. In **Ultra-compact**, text and icons get a size smaller too. Every open window follows, and HappyVibe remembers the choice the next time it starts. Click **Comfortable** to go back.

## During a session

You can change either setting in the middle of a session. Nothing restarts, and the agent doesn't notice.

In **Compact** and **Ultra-compact**, the conversation and the message box use more of the window's width, so a wide window shows longer lines and less empty space on the sides. The agent, its model and what a turn costs stay exactly the same. Only how it looks changes.
