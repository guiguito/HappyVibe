Welcome in. HappyVibe puts a coding agent on your computer and keeps you in charge of it: you see what it does, and you decide what it may do. The app says it in four words: "Good vibes, real code."

New here? Start at the top. The first few pages take you from download to the moment you say yes to the agent's first action, one step at a time.

## Find your way

After **Get started** comes **Everyday use**, the parts of the window you work in all day. Then the reference pages follow the app's **Settings** groups, in the order the app shows them.

- [Get started](/docs/install/): install the app, connect a model, run your first session and answer your first approval.
- [Everyday use](/docs/workspaces-and-sessions/): the sidebar and your sessions, the session view, your files and changes, and schedules that run on their own.
- [Models](/docs/models/): pick who the agent talks to, and which model it uses by default.
- [Abilities](/docs/built-in-tools/): what the agent can do, from its built-in tools to memory, plugins, skills, prompts, MCP servers and agents.
- [Control](/docs/system-prompt/): what the agent is told, what it may do on its own, and what waits for your click.
- [App features](/docs/terminal/): the terminal, voice, AI autofill, keyboard shortcuts and privacy.
- [The record](/docs/stats/): your usage, every permission decision, and what changed in each version.
